How far do you want your image to travel?

Send a photograph and choose how far from its source it should go. The price follows the distance: the further the image moves away, the more composition it contains, and the higher the level of work.

Send a gallery

Any number of photos and videos, which you can add in several rounds: an archive, a series of shots, the memories of a period. Choose the files and the starting level, and you receive a preview.

If the total size stays under 7 MB, the files travel with the form. For heavier galleries, and for videos, the list is sent and we write back with a private upload link; or paste a link yourself (WeTransfer, Drive, Dropbox).

If recognisable people appear in the images, their consent is needed. The work remains confidential and is not shown without your consent.

    The certificate

    Every work and every elaboration is delivered with a deed stating what was sold, to whom, and in which copy.

    For catalogue works it can also be registered on a blockchain, at the buyer’s request. For elaborations of personal photos and for pet portraits the certificate is a numbered, signed document, with no need for a digital wallet.
